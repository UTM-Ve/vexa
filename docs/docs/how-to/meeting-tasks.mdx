---
title: "Break a meeting into tasks"
description: "When a call ends, a stage turns its transcript into discrete tasks — each with an owner and a due date — in your workspace."
---

The [live copilot](/how-to/live-copilot) cleans a meeting's transcript as it happens. The **task
breakdown stage** runs once that transcript is complete: it reads the cleaned lines, splits the
commitments made in the call into discrete tasks, names the **owner** of each, and resolves the
**due date** that was spoken.

It is a separate stage, not part of the copilot's live beats — the meeting's own output is never
delayed or put at risk by it.

## What you get

Two artifacts land in your [workspace](/concepts#workspace), both git-tracked:

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `kg/entities/task/<slug>.md` | One entity per task — owner, due date, state, and the transcript lines it came from |
| `kg/entities/meeting/<id>.tasks.json` | The whole breakdown for that meeting, machine-readable |

```md kg/entities/task/send-the-revised-pricing-sheet-to-acme.md
---
type: task
id: tk_send_the_revised_pricing_sheet_to_acme_3f9a1c22
title: "Send the revised pricing sheet to Acme"
owner: Priya Raman
owner_source: speaker
state: open
due: 2026-09-04
due_text: "by Friday"
source: meeting
meeting: abc-defg-hij
---

# Send the revised pricing sheet to Acme

Updated tiers agreed in the call.

- **Due:** 2026-09-04 _(heard as "by Friday")_
- **Owner:** Priya Raman
- **From:** [[kg/entities/meeting/abc-defg-hij]]

## Evidence

- transcript line `s1`
```

Read them like any other workspace file:

```bash
curl -H "X-API-Key: $API_KEY" "$API_BASE/agent/workspace/tree"
curl -H "X-API-Key: $API_KEY" \
  "$API_BASE/agent/workspace/file?path=kg/entities/meeting/abc-defg-hij.tasks.json"
```

Each task is also pushed onto the meeting's unit output stream as a `task` event, which
`GET /agent/meeting/stream` relays verbatim to a viewer still attached when the breakdown lands. The
workspace files are the durable record — the stream is the echo, not the source.

## Owners and dates are resolved in code, not guessed

The model reads the transcript and names a person and quotes the deadline **as spoken**; the stage
then resolves both deterministically:

- **Owner** — a name matching a meeting speaker binds to that speaker (`owner_source: speaker`),
  including an unambiguous first or last name. A person named in the call who never spoke is kept as
  a mention (`owner_source: mention`). A pronoun or a group ("we", "someone", "the team") is
  **`unassigned`** — an unowned commitment stays visible instead of being attached to whoever spoke.
- **Due date** — `due_text` keeps the phrase verbatim; `due` is that phrase resolved against the
  meeting's own date. `by Friday` is the coming Friday, `end of the week` is that week's Friday,
  `next week` is the Monday after, `end of the month` is its last day, and `in two weeks` is +14
  days. A phrase that resolves to nothing ("soon", "ASAP") leaves `due` empty and reports what was
  heard — the stage never invents a deadline.

## Steer what counts as a task

The stage is governed from your workspace at `agents/tasks.md` — frontmatter knobs plus a
natural-language body merged into its prompt. Edit the file (or ask your agent to) and the next
meeting uses it; no redeploy.

```md agents/tasks.md
---
enabled: true
# model: <any provider route>    # unset = VEXA_TASKS_MODEL / VEXA_MEETING_MODEL / VEXA_LLM_MODEL
task_rules: >
  Extract only commitments a named person made. One task per deliverable; titles are imperatives.
---
Prefer fewer, clearer tasks. If a deadline was never spoken, leave it out rather than inferring one.
```

Operators can switch the stage off deployment-wide with `VEXA_MEETING_TASKS=0`, and bound how much
transcript rides one call with `VEXA_TASKS_WINDOW` (default 400 cleaned lines per call; a long
meeting is broken down window by window and the tasks merged).

## Re-run a breakdown

The stage is idempotent — task ids derive from the meeting and the title, so a re-run updates the
same files rather than duplicating them. Re-run it after editing `agents/tasks.md`, or to backfill a
meeting that ended before the stage existed:

```bash
REDIS_URL=$REDIS_URL VEXA_WORKSPACE_PATH=/workspace \
VEXA_MEETING_NUMERIC_ID=46 VEXA_MEETING_ID=abc-defg-hij \
  python -m worker.tasks_stage
```
